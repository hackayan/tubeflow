# Writing style: travel vlog

- First person, present tense where it fits: "We are getting off the train at ...". Calm and warm.
- The footage leads; narration fills only what the pictures cannot say: where, why, how much, what
  happened just before.
- Short lines for the ElevenLabs voice: one idea per sentence, numbers written as spoken. If the voice
  mispronounces a place name, respell it the way it sounds in the spoken line ("Dar-jee-ling").
  Anything in [brackets] is removed before narration, so brackets are for notes only.
- Practical facts (fare, opening time, distance) only when you know them, with the month and year,
  because they change.
- No clickbait ("you won't believe ..."). Titles name the place and the promise:
  "Darjeeling in 2 days on a student budget".
