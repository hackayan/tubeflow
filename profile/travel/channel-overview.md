## What it is

Travel vlogs made from my own footage: places, journeys, food and people, filmed as I travel,
including trips for conferences and research. Kept on its own channel so the education channel's
audience gets what it subscribed for, and this one finds viewers who came for travel.

## Formats

- **Long vlogs (8-20 min):** one trip or one place, told as a journey with a beginning and an end.
- **Shorts (under 60 s):** one view, one moment, one tip. Vertical, cut from the same footage.

## Rules

- Real places, real experiences, real costs. No invented prices, timings or claims about a place.
- People who can be recognised appear only if they agreed, or are blurred; no children's faces
  without a parent's agreement.
- Music: only tracks you have a licence for (YouTube Audio Library or similar), or none.
- Respect local rules on filming (temples, airports, military areas) and say so in the video.
- Link the education channel only where it fits naturally (for example a conference trip).
